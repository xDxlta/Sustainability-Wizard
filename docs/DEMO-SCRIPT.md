# Demo script (version 2.3)

Run the wizard locally from the repository folder (`python -m http.server 8080`, then http://localhost:8080/)
in a private browser window so the intro page appears. About eight minutes.

## 1. Frame (30 s)
- A tool for the hotel: three steps, nine answers, every answer changes a number or a product. The person
  using it often prepares the case for the owner, so the result is a business case: payback, yearly saving
  and the calculation path, with plastic and CO2e as the add-on.
- What decides the result: how many bottles the hotel puts out per occupied room and night, and what it
  pays per bottle. Hotels serving branded, foodservice or glass water pay back within a year; hotels serving
  the cheapest mass brands often take much longer.

## 2. Scenario: select-service hotel in Surabaya (3 min)
Indonesia; 250 rooms; occupancy "Not sure" (national average 48.7%); plastic bottles, free; bottles "Not
sure" (3 per occupied room-night); 600 ml; price "Not sure"; areas guest rooms and staff areas; chilled.
- Defaults: the typical price is the median of the mass-market references, Rp2,360 per bottle (retail
  marketplace prices; hotels buying from distributors likely pay less). About 133,000 bottles a year,
  Rp314 million of bottled water. Investment Rp496 million (250 x SMART Classic+ 140.00, the basic tap) and
  service Rp301 million a year (250 x 85.00, one filter change per room and year, as in the Westin
  Singapore case computed with Swisspro). Lead: "Saves about Rp13.1M a year after the service contract."
  Cash payback more than 30 years, 17.1 years including staff time freed. The top tiles show the yearly
  saving, 925 staff hours, bottles and plastic avoided; the full table below keeps every figure, with the
  line "Your proposal can include a service plan for your market."
- The staff-area station is a separate block (1 x CLIMA Versa, Rp12.6 million, service Rp5.0 million a
  year); it is not in the room payback.
- Now open step 2 again and choose "Enter our price": Rp9,750 per bottle (a foodservice brand). Lead: "Pays
  for itself in 6 months, then saves about Rp998M a year." This is the point of the demo: the bottle the
  hotel actually serves decides the case. The foodservice reference (Rp9,000 per 380 ml bottle) and a
  premium reference are listed as options; neither drives "Not sure".
- Optional: occupancy "Not sure", closest match "East Java, star hotels" (49.3%): cash payback 28.8 years.
- Under "More": the arguments start with efficiency and water safety because bottled water is cheap here,
  with the country note "30% of the population uses a safely managed drinking water service (2024)".

## 3. Scenario: five-star hotel in Kuala Lumpur (1.5 min)
Malaysia; 200 rooms; occupancy "Not sure", closest match "Kuala Lumpur 5-star" (71.4%, 2025); plastic
bottles, free; bottles "Not sure" (3); size "Not sure"; price "Not sure" (typical RM0.81, median of the
mass-market references); guest rooms only; ambient.
- About 156,000 bottles a year, RM126,070 of bottled water. Investment RM89,112, service RM54,104 a year.
  Lead: "Pays for itself in 1.2 years, then saves about RM71,966 a year." Including staff time freed:
  1.1 years.
- Show the five-year view (break-even marked) and the per room and month comparison.
- Choose hot and chilled water in step 3 to show the upgrade (CLIMA Versa+) with its own payback below the
  base case.

## 4. Adjust the assumptions (1 min)
- "Bottles used outside guest rooms per day": counts in bottles, plastic, CO2e and staff time, and gives the
  drinking-station block its saving and a combined payback.
- "Extra cost per bottle for delivery, storage and disposal": for island resorts (boat transport, waste
  removal); added to the price in the savings and shown on the owner sheet.
- "In-room service price": one change per room and year is the default; per 2-year contract and per change
  every 9 months are the alternatives.

## 5. Owner sheet and request (1 min)
"Print for the owner (PDF)": lead, tiles, business case, five-year view and calculation path on two A4
pages. Then the request form: hotel, segment, rooms and contact are required; the email draft carries the
same figures.
Choose "Own well or treatment plant" as the water supply: the result asks to check the pre-treatment
(particle and activated carbon filtration). "Mains via storage tank" adds the storage-tank argument under More.

## 6. Transparency (30 s)
Open "Sources": every factor with its source, the public hotel cases behind 3 bottles per room-night, the
bottle price references by tier, the occupancy closest matches.

## Anticipated questions
- "Why 3 bottles?" Nine public hotel cases imply about 470 to 1,460 bottles per room and year, median about
  659, which is about 3 per occupied room-night at 60 to 70% occupancy. Industry case studies that describe
  two bottles a night are kept in the note as the lower view; the hotel can enter its own figure.
- "Why no short payback at the typical price in Indonesia?" Mass-brand water costs about Rp2,400 a bottle
  and the service contract is priced in SGD. Enter the price of the bottle actually served.
- "Why these products?" Only lines of the Hospitality price list, matched on the printed water types; the
  basic tap is the base case, the matching unit an upgrade.
- "Is the CO2 figure complete?" Bottle production only, as stated on the sources page.
