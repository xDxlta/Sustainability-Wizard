## Singapore
- STB/SHA Hotel Sustainability Roadmap (2022): "60% of hotel room stock in Singapore to attain
  internationally-recognised hotel sustainability certification by 2025", "up from less than 10 per cent currently".
  https://www.stb.gov.sg/about-stb/media-publications/media-centre/launch-of-the-hotel-sustainability-roadmap-by-stb-and-sha/
- Roadmap infographic lists an "in-room filtration tap" among its measures.
  https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/1/5254d3e2-12cb-40a0-b744-7df61108506b/Sustainability%20Roadmap%20Infographics_final.pdf
- MOS Low Yen Ling, Hotel Sustainability Conference 2023: as of Aug 2023 "at least 35 hotels, about a quarter of the
  room inventory" had obtained or started pursuing certification.
  https://mti.gov.sg/Newsroom/Speeches/2023/10/Speech-by-MOS-Low-Yen-Ling-at-the-Hotel-Sustainability-Conference-and-Marketplace-2023

## Malaysia
- Malaysia Roadmap Towards Zero Single-Use Plastics 2018-2030 (MESTECC, Sept 2018): vision "zero single-use
  plastics for a cleaner and healthier environment in Malaysia by 2030".
  https://www.pmo.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Malaysia-Roadmap-Towards-Zero-Single-Use-Plastics-2018-2030-m-min.pdf
- Halal certification of water filters is an established practice in Malaysia (JAKIM): Coway Malaysia
  "all water purifier products are certified Halal by the Department of Islamic Development Malaysia (JAKIM)"
  https://www.coway.com.my/halal ; Waterco 2022 "received its Halal Certification from JAKIM for its water
  filtration products" https://www.waterco.ca/en/news-events/press-releases/2022/Waterco%20Malaysia%20Receives%20Halal%20Certification
- Vendor claim (unverified against JAKIM): under the Manual Procedure for Malaysia Halal Certification (MPPHM) 2020
  "all processing aids, including filtration media used in food and beverage operations, must be halal".
  https://bacfree.com.my/2026/02/27/halal-water-filter-malaysia/

## Indonesia
- Bali Governor Regulation 97/2018 targeted plastic bags, styrofoam and straws; upheld by the Supreme Court.
- Bali Circular Letter 9 of 2025 (Governor Koster, April 2025) "bans producing, distributing, and selling single-use
  plastic bottled water under one litre". https://www.socialexpat.net/bali-governor-bans-single-use-plastic-bottled-water-under-one-litre/
- Tap water (PDAM) is treated but generally not drunk directly: 2024 study (Sustainability 16(16):7082) found
  "sporadic microbial contamination was detected due to the depletion of residual chlorine".
  https://ideas.repec.org/a/gam/jsusta/v16y2024i16p7082-d1458770.html

## Philippines
- RA 11898 Extended Producer Responsibility Act (2022): obliged enterprises (P100 million+ assets) must implement
  an EPR programme; recovery targets "50% for the footprint generated in 2025, 60% for 2026, 70% for 2027, and 80%
  from 2028 onward". https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11898_2022.html
- Boracay (Malay, Aklan) Municipal Ordinance No. 386 s. 2018 "prohibits the use of single-use or disposable plastic
  items by hotels, resorts, restaurants and establishments in the accommodation business".
  https://denr.gov.ph/news-events/denr-backs-ordinance-banning-single-use-plastics-in-boracay/
- Metro Manila: DOH confirmed Maynilad tap water "safe and potable"; Manila Water compliant with PNSDW.
  https://www.mayniladwater.com.ph/doh-confirms-potability-of-maynilad-water/

## Filtration grades (CDC)
- "About Choosing Home Water Filters": bacteria need an "absolute pore size of 0.3 micron or smaller"; parasites
  "absolute pore size of 1 micron or smaller". https://www.cdc.gov/drinking-water/prevention/about-choosing-home-water-filters.html
- Yellow Book: microfilters with "'absolute' pore sizes of 0.1-0.4 um are usually effective at removing bacteria".
  https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/preparing-international-travelers/water-disinfection-for-travelers.html
